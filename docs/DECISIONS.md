# Decisions

Locked decisions for Tidaq. Sessions follow these without asking. To change one, add a new entry at the bottom with date, the change and the reason. Never edit an old entry silently.

Last updated: 11 Oct 2026 (kickoff with Zaid).

## Product

| #    | Decision                                                                                                                                            | Why                                                                                                                             |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D-01 | **Name: Tidaq** (tide + Nasdaq: "the stock exchange for hotel prices"). Domain **tidaq.com**. Wordmark lowercase `tidaq`.                           | Short, free .com, matches the price-chart idea and Zaid's investing background.                                                 |
| D-02 | **Product:** price history, low/high signal and checkout-trust score for a specific luxury hotel stay (hotel + dates + guests + options).           | The gap: Google Hotels shows little history, no breakfast/room filters, and its cheapest providers often cost more at checkout. |
| D-03 | **Segment: five-star and luxury hotels only** in v1.                                                                                                | A 5–10 % move is €100+ per stay. Buyers care, and our commission per booking is high.                                           |
| D-04 | **Coverage at launch: DACH + top EU cities:** Berlin, München, Hamburg, Frankfurt, Wien, Zürich, Paris, London, Mailand, Rom, Barcelona, Amsterdam. | Where German-speaking luxury travellers actually go.                                                                            |
| D-05 | **Language: German first, English second.** German at `/`, English at `/en`. Informal "du".                                                         | The German market is the focus; English adds reach at almost no extra cost when built in from day one.                          |
| D-06 | **Monetization v1: free for users + affiliate commission** on outbound booking links. No paywall, no subscription in v1.                            | Lowest barrier while we validate demand. Pro tier or "watch my booking" success fee may come later.                             |
| D-07 | **Target user:** "aspirational luxury traveller", 25–45, books a five-star hotel 1–3× a year, price-aware, research-heavy, DACH.                    | Not the ultra-rich (they book through agents and loyalty programs).                                                             |
| D-08 | **Alerts: email** (double opt-in). Telegram and web push later.                                                                                     | Email is universal and legally clear.                                                                                           |

## Design

| #    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Why                                                                           |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D-20 | **Design direction: Soft Modern.** Rounded, friendly, app-like (lavender/violet accent, Plus Jakarta Sans). Zaid also liked Riviera Editorial; borrow its photo-forward deal cards and sky gradient as accents only. Spec in `docs/DESIGN.md`, reference in `docs/design/reference-homepage.html`. | Zaid's pick from six full-page mockups.                                       |
| D-21 | **Prices in German format:** `1.184 €` (amount, space, €; no cents for totals). English: `€1,184`. Always use `Intl.NumberFormat`.                                                                                                                                                                 | Correct German typography. The mockup showed `€1.184`, which is wrong for DE. |
| D-22 | **Falling prices are green, rising are red.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Falling is good for the user.                                                 |
| D-44 | **Design direction: Clean Premium** (replaces D-20 Soft Modern page by page). Big real photos, warm white, one violet accent for actions only, one font family (Instrument Sans, no thin or italic display type), every hotel with a real photo. **Product terms in English inside German copy: "Price Drop(s)", "Price Alert"** (URLs `/price-drops`, `/price-alert`); everything else stays German with "du". Spec and build plan: `docs/design/PREMIUM.md`. | Zaid, 15 Oct: Soft Modern "looks vibe-coded and cheap", too much text and numbers; picked B from two mockup directions and asked for "Preisstürze → Price Drops". |
| D-45 | **GitHub Actions budget: $5 until 1 Nov 2026; the repo stays private.** CI runs only on pushes to `main` (staging deploy) and by hand; pull requests run no CI. Sessions run `pnpm lint && pnpm typecheck && pnpm test && pnpm build` and the full Playwright suite locally before every merge and merge in batches. No visual-baseline or photo workflows until the budget resets (decided by Zaid, 15 Oct). |
| D-46 | **Price drops are red, the stronger the bigger the drop** (core `dropTier`, tokens `--sale-1…4`): below 20 % white badge with red text, 20–39 % light red badge, 40–59 % solid red, 60 % and more bright red with a glow; the same red step colours the drop as text ("−31 %", "1.525 € weniger") and the drop chart's last step. Same rule on every page; the number is always printed, the colour only adds emphasis. The verdict keeps green for "günstig" (D-22 still holds for verdicts and price charts). Asked for by Zaid (15 Oct: "−70 % must not look like −53 %"; later the same day: "make the sale red and stronger red the more sale it is, not all green and same shade"). |
| D-47 | **Price Drops are trips, not single nights:** 5 or 7 consecutive nights booked on one site (2 adults), searchable by start month and length; the hotel page highlights the cheapest 5 nights and week, the city page the cheapest week. No "Live" pills or pulsing dots anywhere. Decided by Zaid (15 Oct: "no one's gonna fly to Shanghai for one night … at least 4 or 5 or even more nights"; the live pill "looks AI and it's useless"). Built in C-125/C-126. |
| D-48 | **International words, few decisions.** The product says "Price Drop(s)" and "Price Alert" in both languages; never "Preissturz", "Sturz" or "Preissturz-Alarm" (also not in URLs: the radar is `/price-drops`, old `/preissturz` links redirect permanently). Lists don't show walls of choices: filters and sort sit behind **one Filter button** that opens a sheet with a live result count, and chosen filters show as removable tags. Decided by Zaid (15 Oct: "remove the German word Sturz … no one understands it"; "put all of these in one filter button"). |
| D-49 | **No pill selections.** Never a row of pill-shaped chips to choose or filter (Zaid, 15 Oct: "I hate the pill selection"). Alternatives: a sticky text nav with an underline for sections (`SectionNav`), text tabs with an underline for filters (deals), a select or stepper inside the Filter sheet / popovers, plain text lines for facts ("4.–6. Dez. 2026 · 2 Nächte · 2 Erwachsene") and for chosen filters ("7 Nächte · Ersparnis — Zurücksetzen"), text rows for lists of dates. Badges on photos (−36 %, 7 Nächte) stay; they label, they don't select. Search popovers stay compact (dates 600 px, guests 340 px, destination 420 px), each centred over its own field. | Zaid in chat, after the C-125/C-131 pages. Admin pages may keep their own controls. |
| D-50 | **Price Drops are checked live.** A drop found in the night grid is re-asked on its booking site for its exact trip (dates, 2 adults) every 30 minutes inside the 2-minute collect run, before the night grid (up to 12 drops a run, the nights get the requests left; free Xotelo only, ~6,000 calls a day; C-142 — a separate `*/5` cron never fired). Gone (site no longer offers it, or more than 5 % above the drop price) → hidden; only drops confirmed live within the last hour are shown at all (C-144), with the checked price and time ("Geprüft heute, 14:32"). | Zaid, 15 Oct: most drops were no longer bookable when he clicked them; drops are the core feature. |
| D-51 | **LiteAPI is used without waiting for Nuitee's written answer** (B-012, B-021): hotel photos from its content API (credited "Foto: <Hotel>, via LiteAPI", self-hosted) and its rate search where it helps (availability checks). If Nuitee objects, we stop and swap the photos back. | Zaid, 15 Oct: "Just use LiteAPI. If it's a problem they will reach out." |
| D-52 | **Only exact final prices are shown.** Xotelo base rates (without taxes and fees) are used to *find* drops, never shown as amounts. Before a Price Drop is shown, the collector asks Google Hotels via DataForSEO (location Germany) for the exact trip; the page shows that quote (each site's nightly price incl. taxes and fees, the stay total, the site's own link) and the time of the quote. Quotes are renewed every 4 hours; a drop without a fresh quote, or whose quote doesn't fit the live check, isn't shown. Budget: at most 600 tasks a day and $15 a month (`DROP_QUOTE_DAILY_CAP`, `DROP_QUOTE_MONTHLY_USD`). | Zaid, 16 Oct night: "We can't allow ourselves estimates or broad numbers." Evidence: `docs/research/exact-prices.md` (Ritz-Carlton Maldives: Google 7.985 €/night, Agoda 7.981 €). |

| D-36 | **Domain `tidaq.com` registered at Namecheap**, DNS managed by **Cloudflare** (Namecheap nameservers point to Cloudflare). `.com` only.                                                                                                                                                                               | Bought by Zaid on 12 Oct 2026. Cloudflare DNS is needed for Workers custom domains, Resend records and Search Console.    |
| D-37 | **Trademark check for "Tidaq" is clean** (EUIPO, DPMA, WIPO, checked by Zaid on 12 Oct 2026).                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Name is safe to use.                                                                                                      |
| D-38 | **Deploys: staging on every green `main` push; production (tidaq.com) only on a `v*` tag or a manual "Deploy → production" run** until launch. Revisit at C-044 (likely: production on every green `main` push after launch).                                                                                         | C-005 had to choose. Before launch the public domain should only change on purpose; staging gives every merge a live URL. |
| D-39 | **Collector uses DataForSEO Hotel Info by `hotel_identifier` only, normalizes per-night prices to stay totals, and collects by polling** (post 05:00 UTC, collect every 10 min until 07:50 UTC in batches of 30, the Workers free-plan subrequest limit; `tasks_ready`/`task_get` are free). Postback optional later. | C-010: name lookups failed 7/7 and were billed; prices are per night; results were ready in < 4 min.                      |
| D-40 | **No breakfast/room filters in v1 (C-055 dropped).**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | C-010: 0 of 198 room offers mention breakfast; room names only on ad entries. Revisit only with another data source.      |
| D-41 | **Free price source: Xotelo** (TripAdvisor meta search, keyless) while there is no data budget, set by `PRICE_SOURCE=xotelo`; **DataForSEO stays the paid fallback** (`PRICE_SOURCE=dataforseo` after a top-up). A stay's history follows one source (`stay_daily` uses the source of its newest snapshot). Gentle use: daily cap, 400 ms between calls. Still **no scraping** of booking sites or Google (hard rule 2). Supersedes "DataForSEO only" in D-12. | Zaid (13 Oct): survive for free during the market test. Spike `docs/research/xotelo.md`: 39/39 active hotels, ~4 sites per stay, 0 errors. |
| D-42 | **History comes from single nights (night grid).** Every morning the collector asks the price source for **every single night of every active hotel for the next `NIGHT_WINDOW_DAYS` (270 since 15 Oct, B-022; was 120) nights, 2 adults, per platform** (`night_daily`). The history of any stay inside the window is built from it: for each past day, sum the nights of the stay **on the same platform** (a platform counts only if it priced every night), then take the cheapest reviewed platform. The fixed stay grid (D-13, ~11 stays per hotel, real multi-night prices) stays as **ground truth**: it measures every day how close the night sums are to real stay prices, and that number is published (methodology page). The stay a visitor opens or watches also gets a **real stay check** (C-022c1 machinery), which is the price shown as "today" and used for alerts. Night calls are free (Xotelo only, never DataForSEO: 4,680 calls/day would cost ~$110/month there). History before the first collected day does not exist anywhere (no source sells past prices of a future stay), so collection starts as early as possible. | Zaid's product: open Tidaq for any dates (e.g. Four Seasons, 1.–4.12.) and see the price history for exactly those dates per platform. Fixed stays can't do that (5,000 combinations per hotel per day); single nights can (120 per hotel). Spike 14 Oct: 120 nights in 42 s at 8 parallel calls, 0 errors, 91–97 % of nights priced; night sums matched real 2-night prices exactly in 77 % of 157 platform pairs, within 3 % in 85 %; the gap (up to 45 %, Mandarin Oriental Munich) is why real stay checks and published accuracy stay part of the design. |
| D-43 | **Long-term coverage and depth (Zaid, 14 Oct):** all five-star hotels in the **25 most popular cities worldwide**, and data for **every guest count (1, 2, families), room type and board** (room only, breakfast, half board, all-inclusive), so Tidaq can answer "3 people, half board, deluxe suite" and alert on room/board-level drops. Replaces D-04 (DACH + EU launch cities) as the target. Rolled out in batches limited by what the data sources allow for free; per-call pricing at full scale is ruled out. Room/board/occupancy comes from supplier APIs (LiteAPI first, C-072), platform prices from meta-search (night grid, D-42). | Zaid's vision: no one (not even Google Hotels) offers history and drops per room type and board. Research 14 Oct (`docs/research/rate-data.md`): room/board don't multiply calls (one call returns all), dates × occupancies do; free supplier APIs want a reasonable look-to-book ratio, so depth grows with bookings. |
